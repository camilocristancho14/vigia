# Star Buddy v2

The official 2025 Gemini sparkle becomes the character. It uses the logo's exact path, with curved concave sides and pointy tips. Brand color flares sit on the tips (red top, yellow left, green bottom), with a light-blue sheen near the top. The sweet face sits on the blue center. While working it wobbles gently as three tiny red/yellow/green sparkles orbit around it, passing in front of and behind the star. When sleeping it tilts over, breathes slowly and lets out Zz.
