# Float Bot v2

A little bot shaped like the Google Antigravity logo. The outline is traced from the official icon path: an "A" arch with a pointed, rounded top, long legs that flare outward and curl into feet, and a smooth parabolic inner arch. The inner arch sits a bit lower so the face has room on the blue body. The top has a Google-color cap: yellow on the top-left, red on the top-right, green on the left shoulder, and a light-blue sheen down the right side. While working it hovers and bobs, code chevrons pulse at its sides, and red/yellow/green sparkles fall from under the arch like antigravity thrust. When sleeping it lands on its feet, breathes slowly and lets out Zz.
