# Mascot concepts for the macOS menu-bar agent-status app

Six cute vector mascot concepts (viewBox 0 0 64 64, transparent background, no text). Each folder contains `main.svg`, `working_1..5.svg`, `sleeping_1..4.svg` and a `README.md`.

## Gemini / Antigravity

### Spark Ghost (`gemini/spark-ghost/`)

Round ghost in the Gemini blue-to-violet gradient with a four-point sparkle growing from its head. Works behind a tiny laptop, sleeps with floating Zz.

![Spark Ghost](gemini/spark-ghost/main.svg)

### Star Buddy (`gemini/star-buddy/`)

The Gemini sparkle itself as a plump, rounded star with a face. Wobbles while two mini sparkles orbit it at work, tilts and breathes when asleep.

![Star Buddy](gemini/star-buddy/main.svg)

### Float Bot (`gemini/float-bot/`)

Arch-shaped bot inspired by the Antigravity logo, hovering over its own shadow. Bobs and emits code chevrons while working, lands softly to sleep.

![Float Bot](gemini/float-bot/main.svg)

## ChatGPT / Codex

### Blossom Bot (`codex/blossom-bot/`)

A face in the center of six teal petals echoing the OpenAI knot-blossom. Petals spin while working and tuck in when sleeping.

![Blossom Bot](codex/blossom-bot/main.svg)

### Cloud Ghost (`codex/cloud-ghost/`)

Mint-white cloud with a ghost hem and teal outline, nodding to the Codex cloud. Eyes become a blinking `>_` prompt at work, data bits float up its sides, and it sags softly asleep.

![Cloud Ghost](codex/cloud-ghost/main.svg)

### Halo Ghost (`codex/halo-ghost/`)

Charcoal ghost with glowing mint eyes and a spinning three-loop knot halo, in ChatGPT's black-and-white spirit. Thought-dots count up while working; the halo dims and tilts while it sleeps.

![Halo Ghost](codex/halo-ghost/main.svg)
